About Me

Hi there! Welcome to my website.

My name is Zhen Chen, I am currently a Postdoctoral Associate in the Miller Lab at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. My work focuses on studying computational and systems neuroscience. My work investigates how neural circuits support cognition, working memory, and flexible behavior.

I use mathematical analysis, computational modeling, dynamical-systems approaches, and biologically realistic neural-network models to study neural computation.

My research interests include computational neuroscience, systems neuroscience, dynamical systems, working memory, and cognition.
